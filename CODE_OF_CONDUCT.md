# Code of conduct

KerfScript is made by and for people who cut things. Beginners and experts
are equally welcome here.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to code or to
  lasers.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you judge.
- Keep criticism about the work, specific, and useful.
- Credit other people's designs and ideas.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discrimination of any kind.
- Sharing someone's private information.
- Posting designs or files you have no right to share.
- Repeatedly pushing a point after being asked to stop.

## Where it applies

In the issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere you represent
the KerfScript project.

## Reporting

Email **hi@mylasertools.com**. Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers
may remove comments, close threads, or block people who break these rules,
and will tell the person why.
